## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The main KPIs used in this project are:

### 1. Total Customers
Total number of customers available in the dataset.

### 2. Churned Customers
Number of customers who have left the company.

### 3. Active Customers
Number of customers who have not churned.

### 4. Churn Rate

Churn Rate shows the percentage of customers who have left the company.

**Formula:**

Churn Rate = (Churned Customers / Total Customers) × 100

### 5. Retention Rate

Retention Rate shows the percentage of customers who are still with the company.

**Formula:**

Retention Rate = (Active Customers / Total Customers) × 100

### 6. Average Monthly Charges

Average amount paid by customers every month.

### 7. Average Total Charges

Average total amount charged to customers.

### 8. Average Customer Tenure

Average length of time customers have stayed with the company.
